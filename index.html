The ships

Every ship is a 36-frame sprite — frame 00 nose-up, 10° clockwise per frame — plus the plugin's target, shipyard, comm and thumbnail pictures. Three sets: the original for Escape Velocity, the larger, re-rendered fleet for EV Override, and two ships Paul rendered for konex.

Use it in your project

No build step, no npm. Pick a way to reach the files; the library finds the rest relative to itself.

1 · Vendor the folder

Clone or copy the whole pack (with LICENSE) into your site, and use relative paths.

<script type="module" src="vendor/yodacon-ship-pack/lib/yodacon-ship-pack.js"></script>

2 · jsDelivr, pinned

A release tag never changes; CORS headers included, so WebGL works.

<script type="module" src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/yodacon/yodacon-ship-pack@v2.0.0/lib/yodacon-ship-pack.js"></script>

3 · Link to yodacon.org

The newest release, served from the project's own site.

<script type="module" src="https://yodacon.org/ship-pack/lib/yodacon-ship-pack.js"></script>

A sprite in HTML: <yodacon-ship>

<yodacon-ship ship="yodacon" spin></yodacon-ship>
<yodacon-ship ship="dart" edition="override" heading="90" scale="1"></yodacon-ship>

Attributes: ship, edition (conex | override), heading, scale, spin, base. All live.

Canvas 2D: drawShip()

import { loadShip, drawShip } from "https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/yodacon/yodacon-ship-pack@v2.0.0/lib/yodacon-ship-pack.js";

const ship = await loadShip("conex", "yodacon");
drawShip(ctx, ship, headingDeg, x, y, 2);   // centred, pixelated

Where they came from

  1. 1997ConEx 1.2 — Paul Richeson's plugin for Ambrosia Software's Escape Velocity: ten ships for the government of Consolidated Express.
  2. 1998ConEx Override 1.0 — the fleet rebuilt for EV Override: larger sprites, and a new hull, the Callisto.
  3. 1998ConEx GL — Joshua Bussdieker's first standalone ConEx, in C and OpenGL, kept in a private repository before konex. No code survives in public.
  4. 2005konex — Joshua Bussdieker's C++/OpenGL remake, which took Override's ten hulls as its cast, plus Advisor and Citrus, two more ships rendered by Paul Richeson.
  5. 2026Gonex — a Go/Ebitengine port of konex that restores ConEx's own game.
  6. 2026This pack — built by Yodacon (yodacon.org).

How they were made

From the ConEx 1.2 readme — The stuff I used to make this plugin, in order from most important to least important:

Infini-D
to design the ships
EV-Edit
to make everything except missions
Schmelta-V
to make missions
ResEdit
to renumber resources and fix what EV-Edit broke
GraphicConverter
to convert all images to PICT and make the target graphics
Make spïn
to gather the 36 individual PICT files into sprites and masks
an Iomega Zip drive
to store all the files
the Escape Velocity Data file
for its TMPL resources, so ResEdit could edit
Escape Velocity
to test
DOCMaker
for the readme
Bryce 2
for the landing screens

The original readmes: ConEx 1.2 · ConEx Override 1.0.

In 2026 they were extracted with the pack's own pure-Python tools (tools/: BinHex, StuffIt 5 Arsenic and classic method 13, resource forks, QuickDraw PICT).

Made with it: example projects

Complete games built on these sprites, to play and to take apart. Each one is a single folder of plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no build step; copy one and start changing it.

  • DOM + Canvas strategy

    Konquest

    KDE's galactic conquest with its exact rules and three computer opponents. Every fleet in flight is a pack ship turned to its heading, and each turn's news shows the hull's comm portrait.

    Source

  • Canvas arena · Gamepad API

    Gauntlet

    Pick any of the thirteen designs and fight the other twelve, weakest first, ending with the 1998 Yodacon. Every hull flies and fights by its record; two players on keys or gamepads.

    Source

  • Canvas racing

    Rally

    Three laps through gates around the pack's planets against five computer racers; the Defender corners like a dart, the Tomaquad like a barge.

    Source

  • Canvas + DOM escort

    Convoy

    Get a Large Pin freighter through six raids with two wingmen on EV-style orders. The target panel shows each raider's 1997 targeting picture; hails come with comm portraits.

    Source

  • Canvas tower defence

    Picket

    Interdict raiders coming down a hyperspace stream, relay to relay, across eight maps of asteroids, gas pockets and nebulae. Raiders fly on the frame for their heading; your ships turn to their targets.

    Source

  • Pure DOM memory

    Recall

    Match each ship across two of its pictures — sprite, shipyard picture, comm portrait, 1997 targeting picture. No canvas; the spinning sprites are the library's own element.

    Source

  • Canvas scroller

    Blockade

    A vertical scroller where your ship banks through the sprite's frames and raiders fly on theirs; two stages, two capital ships.

    Source

  • Canvas docking

    Dock

    Inertia and a turning Large Pin carrier: ten hulls come home, each by its record, and you dock by matching the carrier's sprite frame.

    Source

  • Canvas arcade

    Crossing

    Frogger with the pack's ships: traffic below, freighters to ride across the ion stream above, everything on frames 9 and 27.

    Source

  • Canvas strategy

    Salvo

    Battleship by the salvo rule with every hull scaled to its berth, a probability-map opponent and the 1997 targeting pictures.

    Source

  • Canvas arcade

    Flak

    Missile Command with the pack: raiders dive on their real headings at a fleet at anchor, and Defender batteries turn on their frames to aim.

    Source

  • Canvas arcade

    Formation

    Snake with the pack: every rescued hull follows your path on the frame for its own heading, all scaled to one length from their sprites.

    Source

  • Canvas puzzle

    Refit

    A match-3 where every ship turns on its frames to face the way it moves; four in a row makes a Yodacon bomb, five a Large Pin.

    Source

  • Canvas + DOM trading

    ConEx Courier

    Escape Velocity in miniature: five systems, five goods, courier missions, pirates flying the Override hulls and a shipyard of the ten 1997 ships.

    Source

  • DOM card game

    Fleet Tactics

    Draft a fleet within a budget and fight card by card; hull, firepower and initiative come from each ship's 1997 record.

    Source

  • Canvas arcade

    Orbital Defence

    Hold a planet against waves of ConEx ships: aim its cannon, buy Advisor patrols, repair between waves.

    Source

  • Canvas physics

    Slingshot

    Gravity golf with the 1997 Dart: pull back, let go, and let the planets bend it through the gate. Six levels against par.

    Source

    Pin Miner

    Source
