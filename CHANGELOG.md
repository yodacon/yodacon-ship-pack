# Changelog

Versions follow the files: a new or corrected picture is a minor release, a
moved or renamed file a major one. A tag's files never change.

## Unreleased

- **Planets**: `planets/<type>/<size>/`, five types (rocky, gas, ice, lava,
  garden) in three sizes, generated by `tools/make_planets.py` and listed in
  `catalog.json`. A first set, lit like the ships, until renders replace it
- **Konquest**: KDE's Konquest with its exact rules; Easy, Medium and Hard
  opponents ordered by a tournament (Hard is BecAI); confirmation beeps;
  the pack's planets; the Yodacon console look
- **Six more games** in `projects/`, each with a play-test that drives the page:
  ConEx Courier (EV in miniature: trade, missions, pirates, shipyard),
  Fleet Tactics (draft-and-fight cards), Orbital Defence, Slingshot,
  Pin Miner (idle) and Cutaway (ship recognition)
- **Pack page**: the Override section shows only its new hull, the Callisto,
  and links the ten re-rendered 1997 designs
- **Examples**: a tutorial note in IEEE form on starting a project with the
  sprites, with listings from the library and a Phaser and a Godot port
- **Tutorial notes 2–7**: each example page (DOM, Canvas, `<yodacon-ship>`,
  three.js, Phaser, PixiJS) ends with an IEEE-style note on how it uses the
  assets, with listings cut from its own source by `tools/example_notes.py`
- **Two more examples**: `examples/svg/` (a nested `<svg>` per ship, its
  `viewBox` on one frame) and `examples/css/` (no JavaScript: two `steps(6)`
  animations, and `mod()` with `:has()` for a held heading), with Tutorial
  Notes 8 and 9; `examples/offscreen/` (the game in a Web Worker, drawing on an
  OffscreenCanvas), Tutorial Note 10
- **Three more games**: Gauntlet (pick any of the thirteen designs and fight
  the rest, ending with the 1998 Yodacon; two players, gamepads), Rally (three
  tracks of gates round the planets, five computer racers) and Convoy (escort
  a Large Pin with two wingmen on EV-style orders; targeting pictures and comm
  hails). The three Pins count as one hull in all of them
- **Pack page**: “Made with it”, the example games as inspiration, Konquest
  first; Konquest's screenshot retaken in its console look
- **Tools**: `tools/drive_page.mjs` plays a page with a script; `--width`
- **Style**: `docs/STYLE.md`, `lib/yodacon-style.css` and `styleguide/`;
  `docs/HOUSE-STYLE.md` for the family with treadcore and paulricheson.com
- **Research**: `docs/research/`, eight dated reports; `BACKLOG.md`

## 2.0.0 — 2026-10-21

**Breaking: the plugins' weapon sprite is gone.** The Missile Repeater art in
ConEx and ConEx Override is not Paul Richeson's, so the pack no longer carries
it:

- removed `conex/weapons/` and `override/weapons/`, and the pictures of the
  weapon from `pictures/` (ConEx 1.2: PICT 210, 211; ConEx Override: 1108, 1109,
  6076, 6077)
- in its place, `weapons/missile-repeater/`: a stand-in missile for testing,
  a plain 16×16 line drawn by `tools/build_pack.py`, turned 10° per frame, with
  sheet, mask and alpha sheet in the ships' layout. `catalog.json` lists it
  under a top-level `weapons`; `loadWeapon(slug, base)` loads it, and the 1.0
  form `loadWeapon(edition, slug, base)` still works

**New: the konex edition.** Two more ships by Paul Richeson, Advisor and
Citrus, which first flew in konex (2005) and were never in a plugin:

- `konex/ships/advisor/`, `konex/ships/citrus/`: 36 RGBA frames (konex's 64×64
  textures, the plugins' rotation order), sheet, mask, sheet with alpha,
  target, shipyard and comm pictures, and `ship.json` with konex's own stats
- `sources/konex/`: konex's original TGA files and `specs.xml`, rehosted from
  yodacon/konex (CVS revision 1.3, 9 August 2005), so the pack still builds
  from `sources/` alone

**Text**

- The readmes and ship descriptions have their spelling corrected; each
  `ship.json` keeps the exact 1997–98 wording in `description_original` and
  `escort_original`, and the archives in `sources/` are unchanged
- In the readmes, the GeoCities home page and mailbox are marked inactive, the
  beta testers' 1997 e-mail addresses are left out, and the 1997–98 terms are
  marked as replaced by the GPL
- The edition READMEs no longer list what is held back from `pictures/`
- ConEx's 1997 title picture and fleet picture lead the README and the
  showcase page, credited to Paul Richeson, 28 November 1997

**Lineage and credit**

- ConEx GL (1998), Joshua Bussdieker's first standalone ConEx in C and OpenGL,
  kept in a private repository before konex
- Advisor and Citrus were rendered by Paul Richeson; konex itself is Joshua
  Bussdieker's

**Fixes**

- The demo flies konex's units and gives a small edition ConEx 1.2's fleet as
  targets; the fleet flyby spaces any number of flights evenly round its lap
  (a third flight would have sat on top of the first)

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-21

First release: Paul Richeson's two Escape Velocity plugins, every ship picture
recovered byte for byte and released under GPL-2.0-or-later.

**Assets**

- `conex/`: ConEx 1.2 (1997, *Escape Velocity*), ten ships (shïp/spïn 166–175)
- `override/`: ConEx Override 1.0 (1998, *EV Override*), eleven ships
  (shïp/spïn 175–185), Callisto included
- per ship: sprite sheet, mask, sheet with alpha, 36 RGBA rotation frames,
  target, shipyard, comm and icon pictures, and `ship.json` (the ship record,
  frame geometry, description, escort line, source resource IDs, and the
  matching konex and Gonex folders)
- per edition: the Missile Repeater weapon sprite, every plugin picture as PNG
  (the landing views included; the Ambrosia logo left out), the original
  DOCMaker readme as Markdown with its pictures, a contact sheet
- `catalog.json` (every ship, for code) and `manifest.json` (SHA-256 of every file)

**Use**

- `lib/yodacon-ship-pack.js`: catalogue loading, frame maths, Canvas drawing,
  and the `<yodacon-ship>` web component, with no dependencies
- `demo/`: a small overhead game to fly any ship
- `examples/`: DOM and CSS, Canvas 2D, web component, three.js, Phaser, PixiJS
- `index.html`: the showcase page, static and self-hostable

**Sources and tools**

- `sources/`: the original `.sit.hqx` archives and their expanded `.sit`
  files, rehosted; the catalogue rows and dated screenshots of the pages that
  listed them; links back to Cythera Guides and Ambrosia Software
- `tools/`: a pure-Python extractor for BinHex 4.0, StuffIt 5 (Arsenic) and
  classic StuffIt (method 13), Mac resource forks and QuickDraw PICTs, and the
  pack builder; `make test` checks it against the original bytes

**Pictures decoded correctly for the first time**

- the Small, Medium and Large Pin shipyard and comm portraits, the Trident's
  shipyard and comm, and the Tomaquad's shipyard picture (ConEx 1.2). They use
  a QuickDraw *device* colour table, which earlier extractions misread as
  solid black
- the Dart and Defender sprites (ConEx 1.2), whose 8-bit masks earlier
  extractions applied inverted
