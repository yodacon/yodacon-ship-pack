    Controls

    ←↑→↓ or WASD turn · Space jump · Enter another game. On a touch screen, swipe the grid to turn and tap it to jump.

    How it works

    Every ship leaves an ion wake. Fly into a wake, a rock or the edge and you are out; be the last one flying to clear the arena. Five arenas, each faster, with three rivals each; three lives.

    Jump: once a round, three cells straight ahead over anything in between. It leaves a gap in your wake that anyone can use. From the fourth arena the rivals have one too.
