Controls

← → or A D steer; the Yodacon never stops. On a touch screen, hold the left or right half of the field. Gamepad: stick or d-pad. Enter or Space starts.

How it works

Every rescued ship joins the line and follows your exact path, drawn on the frame for its own heading, so the formation bends through the frames as it turns. A stranded ship tumbles through all 36 frames while it waits. Each rescue scores ten points for every ship in the line, triple for the Large Pin and the 1998 Yodacon, speeds you up and lays another mine.

The hulls run from 24 to 87 pixels long; here each is scaled to 34, measured from its own sprite, so the line reads as a line.

Ships from the Yodacon Ship Pack by Paul Richeson (ConEx, 1997; ConEx Override, 1998), with konex's Advisor and Citrus, GPL-2.0-or-later — github.com/yodacon/yodacon-ship-pack.
