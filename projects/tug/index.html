How it works

Push every derelict into a berth (the dashed squares). The tug pushes one hull at a time and can't pull, so a hull pushed into a corner stays there. Each derelict turns through its frames to face the way it is pushed.

Tractor pull: hold Shift as you back away from a hull and it comes with you. Two pulls a yard. Every yard can be done without them; par is the fewest pushes.

←↑→↓ or WASD move · Z undo · R restart · Enter next yard. On a touch screen, swipe the yard.
