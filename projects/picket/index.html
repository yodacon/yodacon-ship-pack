How to play

Pick a ship, then click a square beside the lane. Each turns to face what it shoots and fires on the raider furthest along. Space launches the next wave; the station pays between waves. Click a ship to upgrade (U) or sell (X); F doubles the speed. A Large Pin costs the station five hull.

Collapse the stream (C or the button): once a wave, the stream goes down for three seconds. Every raider on it stops dead and nothing comes through the tunnel, while your picket keeps firing.

The raiders come down a hyperspace stream between warp relays; your picket interdicts them on the way. You can't build on asteroids or gas pockets, and nothing fires through a nebula. A raider shot down beside a gas pocket sets it off, hurting everything close by, and the pockets next to it go up a moment later. The pockets fill again each wave.

Ships, portraits, targeting pictures and stats from the Yodacon Ship Pack by Paul Richeson (ConEx, 1997; ConEx Override, 1998), with konex's Advisor and Citrus, GPL-2.0-or-later — github.com/yodacon/yodacon-ship-pack. The station is the pack's generated ice planet.
