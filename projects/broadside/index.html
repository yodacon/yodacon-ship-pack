Broadside

Turn– Yours3/3 Theirs3/3

 

 

Drag back from your ship and let go, like a slingshot: the further, the harder. Or ←→ angle, ↑↓ power, S special, Space fire.

Log

    The sides take turns, each ship in turn. A direct hit does full damage, a near miss half; bigger hulls hit harder. Once a battle each ship has a special: a heavy shell (double damage) from a hull 60 px or more, a spread of three from a smaller one. The planets pull every shell.

    Ships and planets from the Yodacon Ship Pack by Paul Richeson (ConEx, 1997; ConEx Override, 1998), with konex's Advisor and Citrus, GPL-2.0-or-later — github.com/yodacon/yodacon-ship-pack.
