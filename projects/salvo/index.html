        Each turn you fire one shot for every ship you have afloat, and so do they. Ships never touch, not even at the corners. The computer keeps a map of every way its targets could still lie.

        Sensor sweep (S on the board): once a game, read a 3 × 3 patch of pirate space for contacts. It costs no turn; contacts show as amber diamonds. On Admiral the computer fires one extra shot a turn.

        Ships, targeting pictures and the comm portrait from the Yodacon Ship Pack by Paul Richeson (ConEx, 1997; ConEx Override, 1998), with konex's Advisor and Citrus, GPL-2.0-or-later — github.com/yodacon/yodacon-ship-pack.
