Controls

←→ turn · ↑ thrust · Space fire · H or Shift hyperspace · Enter another game. Gamepad: stick, d-pad up or RT thrust, A fire, B hyperspace.

How it works

Big rocks break into two middling ones, middling into two small (20, 50 and 100 points). A Necromancer crosses now and then, firing at random; from wave 4 a Dart does too, faster, and it aims. An extra life every 10,000.

Hyperspace: three jumps a wave, somewhere at random, clear of rocks if it can be. One jump in eight goes wrong.

Every ship is drawn 30 px long, scaled from its own sprite, and turns, thrusts and fires by its record.

Ships from the Yodacon Ship Pack by Paul Richeson (ConEx, 1997; ConEx Override, 1998), with konex's Advisor and Citrus, GPL-2.0-or-later — github.com/yodacon/yodacon-ship-pack.
