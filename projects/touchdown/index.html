How it works

Five worlds: a rocky moon; ice, where the pads are slick; a garden world with wind and air; lava, whose vents throw rocks; and the deck of a Large Pin carrier moving over a gas giant. Then round again, heavier and with narrower pads. Points are 50 plus 10 a second of fuel left, times the pad. Three ships.

Retro rockets: twice a world, kill three quarters of your speed in under half a second.

Thrust comes from each hull's Accel, turning from Maneuver, fuel from Fuel.

Ships from the Yodacon Ship Pack by Paul Richeson (ConEx, 1997; ConEx Override, 1998), with konex's Advisor and Citrus, GPL-2.0-or-later — github.com/yodacon/yodacon-ship-pack.
