Target

In the yard

    Yodacon: four in a row. Swap it, or catch it in a blast, and it clears the 3 × 3 round it.

    Large Pin: five in a row, an L or a T. It clears its whole row and column.

    Scrap order (the button or X, then a ship): once a level, every ship of that kind goes at once, without using a move.

    Asteroid yard: from level 5 asteroids drift in. They can't be swapped or matched; a match or blast beside one breaks it.

    Controls

    Click a ship, then a neighbour, to swap them; or drag one onto its neighbour. On the keys: arrows move, Space picks a ship, then an arrow swaps it. A swap that makes no line swaps back and costs nothing. After six quiet seconds a possible move lights up.
