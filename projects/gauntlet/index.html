The ladder

    Controls

    Player 1: ←→ turn · ↑ thrust · ↓ brake · Space fire

    Player 2: AD turn · W thrust · S brake · F fire

    Gamepads: stick or d-pad to turn, A or RT thrust, X, B or RB fire, Start to continue. Pad 1 flies player 1, pad 2 player 2. Press a button on a pad to connect it.
