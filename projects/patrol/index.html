The pirates

Dart chases you. Necromancer heads four tiles in front of you. Trident aims past you from wherever the Dart is. Tomaquad chases until it gets close, then loses its nerve. From level 4 the 1998 Yodacon leads in the Dart's place, a little faster.

Pods 10, fuel cells 50: they turn the pirates to run, and each one caught doubles, 200 to 1,600. The Large Pin freighter calls twice a level below the hold. A ship every 10,000.

Decoy: once a level, a beacon where you are; for six seconds the pirates go after it instead.

Ships from the Yodacon Ship Pack by Paul Richeson (ConEx, 1997; ConEx Override, 1998), with konex's Advisor and Citrus, GPL-2.0-or-later — github.com/yodacon/yodacon-ship-pack.
