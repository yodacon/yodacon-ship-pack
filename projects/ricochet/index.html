Controls

Move the mouse (or drag a finger), or ←→. Space or a click launches. Shift or C tractor · Enter another game. Gamepad: stick, A to launch, B tractor.

How it works

Every pirate flies nose down on frame 18; the tougher the hull, the more hits it takes (the pips under it). Five formations: the picket line, a chevron, a fortress, an escort, and the Large Pin, which takes fourteen. Then round two, every ship a hit tougher.

Where the ball meets your hull sets the angle out. From the third formation the front rank drops bombs that stun the paddle for a second. Pickups: W escort wings (two Gryphons alongside), M two more balls, L lasers, S a slower ball.

Tractor: three a formation. The next time the ball meets the paddle it's caught; launch it again when you're ready.

Ships from the Yodacon Ship Pack by Paul Richeson (ConEx, 1997; ConEx Override, 1998), with konex's Advisor and Citrus, GPL-2.0-or-later — github.com/yodacon/yodacon-ship-pack.
