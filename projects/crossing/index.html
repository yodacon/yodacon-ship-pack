Crossing

Score0 Lives4 Level1

Choose your fighter

Controls

↑↓←→ or WASD hop one cell. On a touch screen, tap above, below or beside your ship. Gamepad: d-pad or stick.

How it works

The bottom half is traffic: the 1998 Yodacon, Citrus, Defenders, Necromancers and Advisors. Touch one and you are gone. The top half is the ion stream: you can only cross it on the freighters, Tomaquads, Darts, the Large Pin, Yodacons and Callistos, and they carry you with them. Fill all five bays to clear the level; every level the traffic runs faster. Forty seconds a crossing.

Everything sideways flies on frame 9 or 27; your fighter turns to frame 0, 9, 18 or 27 to face its hop.

Ships from the Yodacon Ship Pack by Paul Richeson (ConEx, 1997; ConEx Override, 1998), with konex's Advisor and Citrus, GPL-2.0-or-later — github.com/yodacon/yodacon-ship-pack.
