Choose your ship

Controls

←→↑↓ or WASD fly · Space fire · B smart bomb · Enter another run. On a touch screen your ship follows your finger and fires on its own. Gamepads: stick, A or RT, Y for the bomb.

Smart bomb: once a stage, every bomb on screen is wiped out and every raider is hit six times.

How it works

Your ship flies nose up and banks through frames 35 and 34 (or 1 and 2) as it strafes. Raiders come in on frame 18; Darts and Defenders that dive turn on their real frames to come at you. Pick up P for spread shot and S for shield. Each stage ends with a capital ship: the Large Pin, the 1998 Yodacon, and on the third stage, the Override Line, where Citrus and Advisor fly with the pirates, the Override Callisto.

Ships and stats from the Yodacon Ship Pack by Paul Richeson (ConEx, 1997; ConEx Override, 1998), with konex's Advisor and Citrus, GPL-2.0-or-later — github.com/yodacon/yodacon-ship-pack. The planets are the pack's generated art.
