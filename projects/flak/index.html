Controls

Click (or tap) where the flak should burst; the nearest battery with rounds fires. 1 2 3 (or A S D) fire a chosen battery at the crosshair, Space the nearest. The arrow keys move the crosshair (Shift for big steps).

How it works

Six ships lie at anchor: the Yodacon, Callisto, Large Pin, Tomaquad, Advisor and Citrus. Raiders dive on them on the sprite frame for their line of attack: Tridents, fast Gryphons, Necromancers that split into three Tridents, Darts that take two bursts, and every fifth wave the 1998 Yodacon. Ten rounds a battery a wave; the centre battery's shells are faster. A kill bursts too, so kills can chain.

Rounds left and ships afloat score at the end of each wave. Every 10,000 points a lost ship comes back.

Ships from the Yodacon Ship Pack by Paul Richeson (ConEx, 1997; ConEx Override, 1998), with konex's Advisor and Citrus, GPL-2.0-or-later — github.com/yodacon/yodacon-ship-pack. The gas giant is the pack's generated art.
