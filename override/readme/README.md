## Dart

![Dart, picture 2007](pictures/2007.png)

![Dart, picture 2015](pictures/2015.png)

ConEx Dart  
The Consolidated Express Dart Freighter is the most massive ship ever built. It was designed at the end of the 21st century. But when ConEx put the ship out on the market, there was a high demand. The Dart Freighter's best defence is that it can outrun most ships, the only problem is its terrible acceleration.

## Gryphon

![Gryphon, picture 2009](pictures/2009.png)

![Gryphon, picture 2017](pictures/2017.png)

ConEx Gryphon  
The Consolidated Express Gryphon was originally designed as a carried fighter ship. Until the administration noticed that it was a very sturdy ship. Then they enlarged the ship by a factor of 3. It now holds a crew of 25, not 2 as the original.

ConEx Trident

## Necromancer

![Necromancer, picture 2011](pictures/2011.png)

![Necromancer, picture 2019](pictures/2019.png)

ConEx Necromancer  
The Necromancer is a highly classified experimental government project. It is equipped with three Consolidated Arms Multi Phase Dimension Missile Repeaters (CAMPDeMR). The weapon is mounted on top of your ship's massive power cells. It converts your ship's energy into Consolidated Arms Multi Phase Dimension Mk. 83 smart guided missiles.

## Yodacon

![Yodacon, picture 2012](pictures/2012.png)

![Yodacon, picture 2020](pictures/2020.png)

ConEx Yodacon  
The Consolidated Express Yodacon is the Biggest/Baddest ship of the ConEx fleet. This escort ship carries 15 tridents that are stored in 5 launch bays, for fast launching.
