## Takeaways

1. **Planet conquest is the core of the genre.** Seven of the 36 are some form
   of "own the planets on a map". Konquest, the KDE game of exactly that, is a
   compact, rules-first version of it.
2. **Short sessions win.** "30 minutes flat", "60 minutes flat", "quick and
   light". A Konquest game is ten to thirty turns.
3. **Orbital tower defence** is a clean second game for the pack: one planet,
   the Bryce views as a backdrop, and every ship in the pack as an attacker.
4. **The comm and yard pictures are card art.** A deck-builder or a
   fleet-tactics game can show a ship's comm portrait on its card and its
   sprite on the board, which no other free sprite set offers.

## Assets this points to

- Planet sprites in several types and sizes (again)
- A star-map background: a starfield in two or three parallax layers
- Small icons: fleet count badges, production, ownership colours
- Explosion frames for fleet battles
