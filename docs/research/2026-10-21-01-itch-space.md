## Takeaways

1. **Short, complete strategy sells.** "30 minutes flat" (Slipways) and the
   classic 4X remakes are a strong lane, and they need planets more than they
   need animation. That is Konquest's lane.
2. **The browser is normal.** Fifteen of 36 top space games run on the page.
   Everything the pack makes should stay playable in a tab.
3. **Rules can carry thin art.** A terminal 4X (Riftborne) and a text
   survey game (Seedship) chart well; the pack's ships plus simple planets are
   more than enough art for a strategy game.
4. **Arcade and idle are the other two lanes**: a top-down shooter (Starblast)
   and an incremental miner (Astro Prospector). Both are sprite games the pack
   already supports.
