# Used in — the leaderboard

Every game, mod, demo or video known to fly the Yodacon Ship Pack, ranked by
how many of the **eleven ship designs** it flies: the ten 1997 hulls plus the
Callisto. Either edition counts; ties go to whoever was listed first.

Want your project here? See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md): one issue form,
and a reviewer adds the row.

## Where it came from

Not ranked, because they are the pack's ancestry rather than its users:

| Year | Project | By | What it did with these ships |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1997 | **ConEx 1.2**, *Escape Velocity* plugin | Paul Richeson | Designed, rendered and published ten of them |
| 1998 | **ConEx Override 1.0**, *EV Override* plugin | Paul Richeson | Rebuilt them larger and added the Callisto |
| 2005 | **konex**, C++/OpenGL remake | Joshua Bussdieker | Took ConEx Override's ten as its cast, and added Advisor and Citrus |
| 2026 | **Yodacon**, the ConEx revival | Team Yodacon | Recovered every picture byte for byte and built this pack |
